About Us

Building Competent Safety Professionals

Kigali Safety OSH Training Center (KSOHTC) is a professional training institution based in Kicukiro, Kigali. We dedicate ourselves to thoroughly advancing occupational health and safety across major operational sectors. We build competent safety professionals designed to drastically reduce workplace hazards and maintain uncompromised, regulation-aligned safety environments.

Our institute specializes in delivering highly practical, industry-focused safety competency. We cater to individuals, private organizations, and corporate bodies navigating Rwanda’s rapidly expanding heavy industry and infrastructure projects.
